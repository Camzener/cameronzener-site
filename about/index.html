Cameron Zener

Fashion // Leadership

Cameron Zener is a New York based fashion model represented by IMG Models in New York, Paris, Milan, London and Los Angeles. Since 2023 he has walked for Coach and Off-White and shot for Vogue China, WSJ and MAC Cosmetics, while studying economics at NYU.

Fashion is a business as much as an image, and he works on both sides of it.

How I Work

I signed with IMG in 2023, and since then I've worked in five cities, walking for Coach, Off-White, JW Anderson and Dsquared2 and shooting for Helmut Lang and Vogue China, all while studying economics full time at NYU.

Modeling showed me the industry from the inside, and it made me want to understand the part that makes the decisions. That curiosity took me into equity research at Seaport, working with creators at Tinker Taylor, and bringing luxury executives in to talk with students through LARA.

Right now I'm the medium a brand speaks through. Next, I want to be the one building it, and to make a real difference for the people I live and work around.
